Objective: optional product subcategories used only as a filter (public menu, Mostrador quick order, table rounds), plus an improved products screen. ## Specs

- S1 — Subcategories: "cambiar y adaptar bien la parte de productos y también de categorías y agregar subcategorías, así puedo tener Empanadas > Clasicas, Especiales / Pizzas > Especiales, Comunes / Bebidas > Con alcohol, sin alcohol". A category owns zero or more subcategories (name, order, active). The same name may exist under different categories ("Especiales" under Empanadas and Pizzas); names are unique within one category. Managed by `catalog.manage` from the products screen.
- S2 — Only a filter: "La subcategoría es solamente como un filtro, nada más, no por tema precios ni nada de eso." Prices, the combined dozen and inventory consumption do not change; a combined dozen can mix subcategories. Sellability does not depend on a subcategory: an inactive subcategory only stops being offered as a filter and for new assignments; its products stay sellable and visible under their category.
- S3 — Optional: a product can belong to a category without a subcategory; a category can have no subcategories. A product's subcategory must belong to the product's category (validated by the backend and enforced by PostgreSQL).
- S4 — Filter in the 3 places: "El filtro va en los 3 lugares: menú público, pedido rápido (Mostrador) y rondas de mesa." After choosing a category that has active subcategories with products, a second row offers "Todas" plus those subcategories.
- S5 — Products screen: "si tu consideras cambiar algo hazlo". Improve it with judgement and justify each change in the Log.
- S6 — Production migration: additive, keeps every current product and category. Rollback refuses once subcategories exist.
- S7 — Document what happens when a parent category is deactivated (see Log L2).
- S8 — Tests: own PostgreSQL container with its own port and database (never `testing_playwright` or dev/prod); e2e specs on desktop and mobile; before/after screenshots in the scratchpad; full PHPUnit suite in the `sail-8.5/app` image.

- L2 — Design. A separate `product_subcategories` table (not a self-referencing `categories.parent_id`) keeps `products.category_id` as the only sellability and ordering key, so the pricing engine, checkout resolution, inventory and every existing category query stay untouched. `products.subcategory_id` is nullable; the composite foreign key `(subcategory_id, category_id) → product_subcategories (id, category_id)` makes a cross-category assignment impossible even through direct SQL, and is skipped when the subcategory is null. A subcategory cannot move to another category (its products would break the key); rename or deactivate it instead. No delete in this release, consistent with categories. Existing rows get `subcategory_id = null`: nothing is rewritten. Seeds: none — which empanadas are "Clásicas" is the user's call, made in the UI.
  Deactivating a parent category: its products stop being sellable and disappear from the public menu, Mostrador and table rounds exactly as before (the category's `is_active` is the only gate); its subcategories keep their own `is_active` and assignments untouched and are hidden with it. Reactivating the category brings back the products and their subcategory filters as they were. Deactivating only a subcategory hides that filter chip; its products stay sellable under the category's "Todas".
